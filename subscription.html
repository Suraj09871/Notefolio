Frequently Asked Questions

How long is my subscription valid?

All subscriptions are valid for 1 year from the date of purchase. You can renew your subscription before it expires to maintain uninterrupted access.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll pay the difference between your current plan and the new plan, and your subscription period will remain the same.

Can I download notes on all plans?

No, download capability is only available on the Premium plan. Basic and Standard plans allow online reading only.

What happens when my subscription expires?

When your subscription expires, you'll lose access to the notes included in your plan. Any notes you've purchased individually will still be accessible.
