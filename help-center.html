How Can We Help You?

Find answers to common questions, get support, or reach out to our team for assistance with any issues.

Orders & Payments

How do I place an order?

To place an order on NoteFolio:

  1. Browse our collection of notes or use the search function to find specific topics
  2. Click on the note you're interested in to view more details
  3. Click the "Add to Cart" button
  4. Review your cart and proceed to checkout
  5. Select your preferred payment method
  6. Complete the payment process

Once your payment is confirmed, you'll receive an email confirmation, and the notes will be accessible in your "My Notes" section immediately.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept various payment methods including:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay)
  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
  • Net Banking (All major Indian banks)
  • Razorpay gateway for secure transactions

All payments are processed securely through our payment partners, ensuring your financial information remains safe.

How can I track my order?

You can track your orders in the "My Orders" section of your account. This shows all your past orders and their current status.

Since our products are digital, most orders are processed instantly. If you encounter any issues with accessing your purchased notes, please contact our support team.

How do I apply a coupon code?

To apply a coupon code:

  1. Add notes to your cart
  2. Go to the cart page
  3. Find the "Apply Coupon" section
  4. Enter your coupon code and click "Apply"

The discount will be automatically applied to your order total. Some coupons have minimum purchase requirements or are specific to certain categories of notes.

Is my payment information secure?

We take security very seriously. All payment information is processed through secure payment gateways that use industry-standard encryption protocols. We never store your complete credit card information on our servers.

Our website uses SSL (Secure Socket Layer) technology to ensure that all data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted and secure.

Notes & Downloads

How do I access my purchased notes?

After purchasing notes, you can access them in the "My Notes" section of your account. You have two options:

  1. Read Online: Click on any note to open it in our online reader with features like highlighting, bookmarking, and note-taking.
  2. Download: Click the "Download" button to save the note to your NoteFolio library for offline access within our platform.

Your purchased notes remain accessible for 1 year from the date of purchase.

Can I download notes to my device?

For copyright protection and to respect our content creators' intellectual property, notes can only be downloaded within our platform and cannot be saved to your device's storage.

When you download a note, it becomes available in your "Downloads" section for offline access within the NoteFolio platform. This ensures our content remains protected while still allowing you to study without an internet connection.

How long can I access my purchased notes?

You can access your purchased notes for 1 year from the date of purchase. After that period, you may need to repurchase them if you still need access.

The expiration date for each note is clearly displayed in your "My Notes" section, with reminders when notes are approaching expiration.

What features does the note viewer offer?

Our advanced note viewer includes several features to enhance your studying experience:

  • Highlighter Tool: Mark important sections with customizable color highlights
  • Bookmarking: Save specific pages for quick access
  • Note-Taking: Add your own notes alongside the content
  • Table of Contents: Easy navigation through chapters
  • Search: Find specific content within notes
  • Zoom Controls: Adjust text size for comfortable reading

These features are designed to make your studying more efficient and effective.

Are the notes regularly updated?

Yes, our notes are regularly reviewed and updated to ensure accuracy and relevance. When a note you've purchased receives a significant update, you'll be notified via email.

Minor updates are made automatically and don't require any action on your part. For major content revisions, you'll have access to both the original version you purchased and the updated version.

Account & Profile

How do I create an account?

Creating an account on NoteFolio is quick and easy:

  1. Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner of the page
  2. Fill in your details (name, email, and password)
  3. Click "Sign Up" to create your account
  4. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox

Once your account is created, you can start browsing, purchasing, and accessing notes immediately.

How do I reset my password?

If you've forgotten your password:

  1. Click on the "Login" button
  2. Click on "Forgot Password" below the login form
  3. Enter your email address and click "Reset Password"
  4. Check your email for a password reset link
  5. Click the link and follow the instructions to create a new password

The password reset link is valid for 24 hours. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact our support team.

How do I update my profile information?

You can update your profile information in the "Edit Profile" section of your account:

  1. Log in to your account
  2. Click on your name or profile icon in the top right corner
  3. Select "Edit Profile" from the dropdown menu
  4. Update your information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  5. Click "Save Changes" to apply the updates

You can also change your password from this section by providing your current password and entering a new one.

How do I delete my account?

If you wish to delete your account:

  1. Log in to your account
  2. Go to "Edit Profile"
  3. Scroll to the bottom and click "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your decision by entering your password

Important: Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data, including purchase history and access to purchased notes. This action cannot be undone.

If you're experiencing issues with our service, please contact our support team before deleting your account—we might be able to resolve your concerns.

Anti-Screenshot Protection

How does anti-screenshot protection work?

Our anti-screenshot protection uses advanced techniques to prevent unauthorized copying of premium content:

  • Dynamic watermarking with your personal information
  • Detection of screenshot attempts
  • Blocking of common screen capture methods
  • Content protection when printing or using browser tools

When a screenshot attempt is detected, the content is temporarily hidden and a warning is displayed. This helps protect the intellectual property of our content creators.

Why can't I take screenshots of the notes?

Screenshots are disabled to protect intellectual property rights and prevent unauthorized distribution of premium content. This allows us to:

  • Ensure fair compensation for content creators
  • Maintain the quality and exclusivity of our offerings
  • Prevent copyright infringement
  • Keep prices affordable for all students

While you cannot save or screenshot the content to your device, you can access it anytime through our platform and use our built-in note-taking tools to mark important sections.

How can I study without taking screenshots?

We understand the need for effective study methods. Instead of screenshots, we offer these alternatives:

  • Highlighting: Use our built-in highlighter to mark important content
  • Notes: Add your own notes alongside the content
  • Bookmarks: Save important pages for quick access
  • Offline Access: Download notes within our app for studying without internet
  • Export Highlights: Export your highlights and notes as text files

These tools are designed to give you a superior study experience while respecting intellectual property rights.

I'm getting false screenshot detection alerts

If you're experiencing false positives (alerts when you haven't attempted to take a screenshot):

  1. Make sure you're using a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  2. Disable browser extensions that might interfere with our platform
  3. Try using a different device if possible
  4. Clear your browser cache and cookies
  5. Update your browser to the latest version

If the issue persists, please contact our support team with details about your device, browser, and when the false alerts occur. We're constantly improving our systems to minimize false positives.
